Teaching Philosophy

I believe in creating an engaging learning environment that bridges theoretical knowledge with practical applications. My approach focuses on interactive teaching methods, real-world problem-solving, and fostering critical thinking skills. I strive to make complex concepts accessible through hands-on activities and collaborative learning experiences.

Approach & Methods

Interactive learning

Utilizing gamification tools like Kahoots! and Padlet to increase engagement and facilitate active learning in both online and in-person environments.

Hands-on experience

Emphasizing practical application through lab activities, real-world projects, and industry-relevant tools and technologies.

Collaborative learning

Fostering teamwork and peer learning through group projects, discussions, and collaborative problem-solving activities.

Continuous assessment

Providing regular feedback and assessment to help students track their progress and identify areas for improvement.
