Current Research

Ibanag Speech Data Collection

I am collecting Ibanag speech data to support natural language processing and speech recognition research for the Ibanag language. This project aims to preserve and document Ibanag language resources while enabling the development of AI-powered tools for the Ibanag-speaking community.

Data Collection Goals:

  • Build a comprehensive speech corpus for Ibanag language
  • Support development of speech recognition systems
  • Enable text-to-speech applications in Ibanag
  • Contribute to language preservation efforts

If you speak Ibanag, please help me collect data here.
